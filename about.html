Our Story & Heritage

Where Floral Art Meets Fine Patisserie

Born in Nashik, The Flora Bakery was founded with a singular devotion: crafting 100% pure vegetarian, eggless confections crowned with organic edible blooms.

Handcrafted in Nashik

A Symphony of Organic Blooms & Delicate Flavours

At The Flora Bakery, every cake is a botanical canvas. We believe celebrations deserve more than ordinary sugar frosting — they deserve the natural elegance of fresh organic rose petals, soothing French lavender, golden chamomile, and 24-karat pure edible gold leaf.

From our dedicated kitchen studio in Sai Nath Nagar, Nashik, our master pâtissiers handcraft small-batch chiffon sponges, French craquelin choux, and gourmet gifting hampers. Every recipe is meticulously perfected to be 100% Pure Vegetarian and Eggless without ever compromising on feather-light texture or luscious moisture.

Our Four Botanical Pillars

The principles guiding every creation that leaves our ovens.

100% Pure Vegetarian & Eggless

Every single bake is certified pure vegetarian. We utilize cultured butter, fresh cream, and organic vanilla without a trace of gelatin or eggs.

Organically Cultivated Blooms

We source food-grade culinary flowers from organic nurseries — free from synthetic pesticides, carefully sanitized, and placed minutes before delivery.

Chilled Doorstep Delivery

Our dedicated temperature-controlled vans ensure your delicate cakes arrive in pristine, chilled condition anywhere across Nashik city.
