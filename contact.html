Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance do I need to order?

For standard menu items, we offer Same-Day Chilled Delivery in Nashik on orders placed before 3:00 PM. For multi-tiered or bespoke customized wedding cakes, we recommend placing orders 24–48 hours in advance.

Are all creations 100% Pure Vegetarian and Eggless?

Yes, absolutely! 100% of our products are purely vegetarian and eggless. Our entire bakery kitchen is dedicated eggless certified, using only cultured butter, pure dairy cream, and natural botanical extracts.

Are the floral blooms truly safe to eat?

Yes! We work directly with certified organic nurseries cultivating culinary edible blooms (rose petals, lavender, chamomile, violas, pansies) that are 100% pesticide-free and food-safe.
